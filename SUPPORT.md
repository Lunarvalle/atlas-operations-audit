# Support and availability

Paid access is not publicly open. Do not send payment, bank details, credentials or confidential records through GitHub.

For a reproducible defect or compatibility question, open an issue in this repository. Include product ID, local version, browser/CLI/MCP path, expected and observed results, and a synthetic minimal input. Remove names, email addresses, account numbers, tokens and proprietary records. Do not upload customer files.

Product feedback is not an order or guaranteed support commitment. No fixed response-time SLA or unlimited custom development is promised. The eventual paid channel will supply purchase and refund terms; mandatory rights remain unaffected.
